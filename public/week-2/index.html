The not-teacher

None of this means we stop teaching. Jesus didn’t abolish teachers in Matthew 23. He moved every one of us into the ranks of the siblings, under the one Teacher who is the Christ. We still have something to tell: the best news there is. But we didn’t invent it, we didn’t earn it, and we haven’t finished learning it (1 Corinthians 4:7).

Skeptics can tell the difference between someone who is selling and someone who is sharing. Be firm on Christ. Be open to reason about almost everything else. Ask real questions, and listen to the whole answer. We all might learn something.
