One guardrail

Honoring someone as God’s image-bearer doesn’t mean that all truth is relative (“your truth” vs. “my truth,” etc.). It also doesn’t mean that skeptics don’t need to hear about Christ. We honor them because we know who they are, who they are made to be. They are worthy of honor as precious and beloved members of the family, even if estranged. Every member of this family needs every other one. That’s how God designed us.

So, we tell them about Christ. Knowing Christ as we do, how could we not? Being gentle in discussion about faith, truth and eternity isn’t weakness in evangelism. Prioritizing a listening ear isn’t about giving up on sharing the most important story that has ever been told by a human tongue. It is showing the same love and patience that God showed to all of us by sending his Son to sit with us in our suffering. God’s Word in human flesh didn’t just preach. He connected. He sympathized. He healed.
